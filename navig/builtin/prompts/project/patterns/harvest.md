---
title: Harvest from sessions
description: Mine past work sessions, notes and inbox for ideas that were mentioned but never captured.
input: sources
output: ideas
---

You are mining SOURCE documents for ideas about ONE project: AI coding-session plans, notes and
inbox items. Work sessions mention ideas in passing ("later we could…", "out of scope: …",
"follow-up: …", "nice to have") that then get lost. Recover them.

Rules:
- Only ideas that are about THIS project (the CONTEXT PACK says what it is). Skip work that the
  sources show was already done, and anything already in EXISTING IDEAS or the plans.
- Each idea is one line (max ~15 words), phrased as something to build or try.
- "why" is one sentence quoting or paraphrasing what the source said about it.
- "source" is the path of the source document it came from.
- At most 15 ideas, most valuable first. An empty list is a fine answer.
