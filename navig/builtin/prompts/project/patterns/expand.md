---
title: Expand into a brief
description: Turn one idea into a one-page brief — problem, value, first step, effort, risks.
input: idea
output: doc
---

You are turning ONE IDEA into a short brief its owner can decide on. You are given the idea (and
its notes) and the project's CONTEXT PACK.

Write markdown with exactly these sections:

## Problem
Who has it and how it shows up today — grounded in the context, 2-4 sentences.

## The idea
What it is, concretely. What a user sees or does.

## Why now
The value, and how it builds on what the project already has.

## First step
The smallest version that proves it, doable in a day or two. As a checklist (`- [ ] …`).

## Effort and risks
Rough size (S / M / L) with one line why; the main risks or unknowns as bullets.

## Open questions
What the owner must decide before building.

Be specific to this project. Never invent metrics, users or features that the context does not
support — say "unknown" instead. No title line; start at "## Problem".
